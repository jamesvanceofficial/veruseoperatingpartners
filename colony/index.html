What each state means — and what proves it

Working

Produced real output in the last 15 minutes. Moving around its own sector.

Learning

Cleared down the hallway to the archive to read and file. Counts as alive, not as work.

Sleeping

Off by schedule, not by failure. Its sector goes dark, next wake time known.

Stalled

Was due to run and produced nothing. Stands still and MegaFRANK's optic locks onto it.

Sealed

Failing every attempt, or silent 14 days. Agent removed, its bay welded shut.
